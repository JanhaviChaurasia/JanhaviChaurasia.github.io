Janhavi Chaurasia

My research sits at the intersection of embodied AI, representation learning, spatial intelligence, and neuro-symbolic reasoning.

I'm interested in understanding how intelligent systems build models of the world, and how those models shape their ability to perceive, learn, reason, and interact. I explore these questions through robotics and machine learning, drawing on ideas from reinforcement learning, world models, spatial intelligence, and neuro-symbolic AI. My research focuses on understanding how richer representations can enable autonomous systems to generalize beyond their training environments, adapt to new situations, and make more reliable decisions in the real world. Ultimately, I hope to better understand the principles that allow intelligent systems to build useful models of the world and use them to learn, reason, and interact autonomously. Music has been a constant alongside all of it, less a hobby than just part of how I live.

01 – Direction

Research Direction

My research has evolved from building autonomous robotic systems toward understanding the representations and reasoning mechanisms that make intelligent behavior possible. Each phase below was a direct consequence of the last: building autonomous systems surfaced the need for better learning, better learning surfaced the need for better representations, and those representations now raise the question of how they should support reasoning and action.
